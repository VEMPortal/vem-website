PSN Top Guns Recognition — Q2 2026

The Large Cap Value Equity strategy received four PSN Top Guns recognitions for the quarter ended June 30, 2026: 5 Star Top Gun and Bull & Bear Masters in the Large Value Universe, and 5 Star Top Gun and Bull & Bear Masters in the US Value Universe. Two screens, two different questions

The two categories do not measure the same thing, which is why earning both is worth a paragraph.

The 5 Star Top Gun screen asks whether a strategy has been consistent. PSN requires returns above the style benchmark across the three most recent rolling three-year periods, an r-squared of 0.80 or greater against that benchmark over five years, and five-year volatility at or below the peer group median. The ten best three-year returns that clear all of it become 5 Star Top Guns.

The Bull & Bear Masters screen asks how a strategy behaved in each direction. PSN requires an upside capture ratio above 100 and a downside capture ratio below 100 relative to the style benchmark over a three-year period. The ten best ratios of upside capture to downside capture become the Bull & Bear Masters.

Neither screen carries the other. A strategy clears each one on its own terms, and the Large Cap Value strategy cleared both in two separate universes. Full methodology appears below.

PSN Award Methodology

Through a combination of PSN’s proprietary performance screens, the PSN Top Guns awards products in six proprietary categories in over 75 universes based on continued performance over time.

5 Star Category

Had an r-squared of 0.80 or greater relative to the style benchmark for the recent five-year period. Moreover, the strategy’s returns exceeded the style benchmark for the three latest three-year rolling periods. Products are then selected which have a standard deviation for the five-year period equal or less than the median standard deviation for the peer group. The top ten returns for the latest three-year period then become the 5 Star Top Guns.

Bull & Bear Masters

Had an r-squared of 0.80 or greater relative to the style benchmark for a three-year period. Moreover, the strategy had an upside market capture over 100 and a downside market capture less than 100 relative to the style benchmark. The top ten ratios of Upside Capture Ratio over Downside Capture Ratio become the PSN Bull & Bear Masters.
