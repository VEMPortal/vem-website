Who we are

Independent by design.
Fiduciary by commitment.

Founded in 2015, Vann Equity Management is an independent, SEC-registered investment advisory firm built on a single principle: the client’s interest comes first. So strong is our commitment that we have adopted fiduciary standards across the entire firm.

We manage multiple equity and tactical asset-allocation portfolios for high-net-worth individuals, institutions, endowments, foundations, and family offices — each guided by the same disciplined, proprietary process.

What we manage

Two ways to invest with us.
One disciplined process.

Every VEM portfolio — built security by security or through cost-efficient ETFs — runs through the same proprietary, risk-controlled process.

  • We target large U.S. companies whose earnings, sales, and margins are accelerating faster than the market. Names are surfaced by our proprietary quantitative screens, then validated by the investment committee for the durability of that growth — typically leaders across technology, consumer, and healthcare.

  • Quality large-caps trading below our estimate of intrinsic value, with a fundamental catalyst for re-rating. We favor strong balance sheets and improving returns on capital — companies the market has temporarily mispriced, not permanently impaired.

  • Developed- and emerging-market leaders held through U.S.-listed American Depositary Receipts — global exposure without the friction of foreign settlement. The same conviction-driven selection process, applied to an international opportunity set.

  • A core bond allocation built from low-cost ETFs, positioned across duration and credit to match the prevailing rate and economic cycle. Designed to provide ballast and income — not to chase yield.

  • Broad U.S. equity exposure assembled from index ETFs and tilted by our multi-factor scoring — a fully diversified domestic core that can be dialed up or down as conditions change.

  • Global ex-U.S. equity exposure spanning developed and emerging markets, delivered through diversified ETFs — geographic diversification that complements the domestic sleeve.

  • A complete, ready-made allocation — equities, fixed income, and alternatives in a single diversified solution. Built for clients who want one professionally managed portfolio that spans every major asset class.

Our process

From nine thousand names
to forty.

Every equity portfolio is distilled through a five-stage process — quantitative, fundamental, and continuous — that narrows a global universe to the few dozen companies we choose to own.

We start with the entire field — roughly 9,000 publicly traded companies across every sector, country, and market capitalization. No watchlist, no benchmark, no inherited positions. Before a single filter is applied, every name is on the board and eligible for consideration.

Proprietary, rules-based screens rank the universe on liquidity and structure, growth and value characteristics, and a multi-factor score. The math is repeatable and free of emotion — it narrows roughly 9,000 names to the top decile, about 1,000 candidates that merit a closer look.

Our investment committee examines the survivors by hand: the durability of earnings, business and competitive (SWOT) analysis, cash-flow quality, and the caliber of management. Judgment is applied where numbers alone fall short, leaving roughly 150–250 semi-finalists.

We assemble our highest-conviction names into a diversified portfolio of 40–60 holdings, governed by country, sector, industry, and position-size limits so no single decision can dominate the outcome. Conviction is always balanced against risk.

Most managers fall in love with their winners. Discipline is knowing when to let go. Quantitative triggers, technical signals, and qualitative review run continuously — governing when we trim or exit a position — so every holding stays under active review for as long as we own it.

What this means for you

You own a focused portfolio of 40–60 companies — each one the survivor of a disciplined, repeatable process of quantitative rigor, fundamental conviction, and continuous risk control. No story stocks, no black boxes. Every position has earned its place.
