Independent by design.
Fiduciary by commitment.

What we manage

Two ways to invest with us.
One disciplined process.

Every VEM portfolio — built security by security or through cost-efficient ETFs — runs through the same proprietary, risk-controlled process.

  • We target large U.S. companies whose earnings, sales, and margins are accelerating faster than the market. Names are surfaced by our proprietary quantitative screens, then validated by the investment committee for the durability of that growth — typically leaders across technology, consumer, and healthcare.

  • Quality large-caps trading below our estimate of intrinsic value, with a fundamental catalyst for re-rating. We favor strong balance sheets and improving returns on capital — companies the market has temporarily mispriced, not permanently impaired.

  • Developed- and emerging-market leaders held through U.S.-listed American Depositary Receipts — global exposure without the friction of foreign settlement. The same conviction-driven selection process, applied to an international opportunity set.

  • A core bond allocation built from low-cost ETFs, positioned across duration and credit to match the prevailing rate and economic cycle. Designed to provide ballast and income — not to chase yield.

  • Broad U.S. equity exposure assembled from index ETFs and tilted by our multi-factor scoring — a fully diversified domestic core that can be dialed up or down as conditions change.

  • Global ex-U.S. equity exposure spanning developed and emerging markets, delivered through diversified ETFs — geographic diversification that complements the domestic sleeve.

  • A complete, ready-made allocation — equities, fixed income, and alternatives in a single diversified solution. Built for clients who want one professionally managed portfolio that spans every major asset class.
